This is an untested audience hypothesis, not evidence of channel demand. Lead with the evidence checklist; offer the existing eligibility request as a secondary action. ## Draft A — standalone educational post

For the owner to review and, if appropriate, publish manually. Do not use on HN. The affiliation sentence must be true for the publishing account.

> Before paying for an SEO/GEO audit, ask four questions:
>
> 1. Which public pages were sampled, and when?
> 2. Which exact observation supports each proposed change?
> 3. How would you verify the fix, and when should you leave the page alone?
> 4. Which outcomes are still unmeasured?
>
> A crawler directive is evidence about a policy. It does not prove a ranking or an AI citation. A collector limit is a limit of the audit, not automatically a defect in your website.
>
> I’m sharing this as the operator of the Audit GEO pilot. The pilot samples initial public HTML; JavaScript rendering, search-position measurement and consumer AI mention testing are outside its current scope.
>
> Requests are open for eligibility review. Payment is only invited for useful, reviewed work when delivery capacity is confirmed. Save the private status page; email notifications are not currently promised.
>
> Scope and illustrative example: https://audit.vutler.com/

The link intentionally uses the existing public homepage. The guide is NOT deployed; do not link to its pending route. No private report or customer finding is included.

## One bounded measurement

After explicit channel selection/authority, publish only one reviewed version. Record the public post URL, publication date, chosen text and the platform's aggregate impressions/link-click totals where available at seven days. Capture anonymous inventory immediately before and seven days after: requests, eligible reviewed reports, verified payments and deliveries, preserving the retained-inventory limitations in FUNNEL-BASELINE.json.

No attribution instrumentation exists yet. Do not label an inventory delta as LinkedIn-acquired demand or divide it by post impressions to report a customer conversion rate. Link clicks/impressions are platform engagement only. If the platform does not expose clicks, report unknown. Do not add UTM collection, tracking scripts or customer source fields as part of this preparation pass. Never save respondent identities or private tracking URLs in the experiment record.

Decision at seven days: no observable clicks → reconsider audience/hook; clicks with no observed new requests → inspect landing/intake; requests with no useful supported work → inspect audience/offer fit; eligible reports awaiting delivery → prioritize fulfillment rather than more promotion. These are diagnostic hypotheses, not predetermined numerical success targets. No repeat campaign is authorized by this kit.
